# Bio — Saurabh Jalendra

Saurabh Jalendra is an AI research engineer working at the intersection of world models, reinforcement learning, and empirical AI safety. He is based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India (IST, UTC+5:30), and operates under the avatar `>_`. He is Co-Founder & AI Lead at SKY Advanced Research LLP (informally "SKY AI"), where the formalized role began Feb 2025 (LinkedIn-canonical); pre-incorporation product work on Klares started Oct 2024.

His M.Tech in AI/ML from BITS Pilani (Work Integrated Learning Programmes, May 2024 – Mar 2026) culminated in a dissertation titled "Quantum-Enhanced Simulation Learning for Reinforcement Learning," defended March 2026. The dissertation ran 200 experiments across 5 methods × 5 seeds × 8 environments. The Interference Ensemble method achieved a 36–47% reduction in world model prediction MSE on state-based DMControl robotics (Walker, Cheetah, Reacher-easy, Reacher-hard) at p<0.008 after Bonferroni correction — three of the four quantum-inspired methods returned null or catastrophically negative results, all of which are disclosed honestly. Hardware: NVIDIA RTX 5090 (classical, not quantum).

Before the AI pivot, Saurabh spent six years (Jan 2018 – Mar 2024) as Director of S K Jalendra Marketing Services Pvt Ltd — the family business — which serves as the bridge between his B.Tech in Computer Science from Government Engineering College, Bikaner (Jun 2013 – Jul 2017) and the present. His ISRO Project Internship at NRSC Jodhpur (Nov 2016 – Apr 2017, concurrent with B.Tech final semester) covered VR heritage visualization (Unity + Oculus Rift + HTC Vive) and Meshroom-based photogrammetry on aerial drone imagery, under mentor Gaurav Kumar. An MBA in Banking and Finance from NMIMS Mumbai (Apr 2022 – Apr 2024) and a Diploma in Import & Export Management from iiiEM (2020) fill in the operations-and-finance side.

Current research arc: from computer vision (B.Tech + ISRO) → applied ML (SKY AI products) → research-engineering (M.Tech dissertation + Simulating Anything). His stated north star is "using AI to understand and simulate the physical world." He tracks V-JEPA, DreamerV3, the JEPA-family SSL line, and Yann LeCun's autonomous-machine-intelligence (AMI) research direction.
